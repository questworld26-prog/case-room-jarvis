## Exhibit 3: Packaging Format Distribution & Returnable Rates (2024)
- **Returnable Glass Bottles:** 48.2% of total packaging volume (Achieving 96.9% collection and refill rate in primary returnable markets such as Germany, Austria, and Mexico).
- **Aluminum Cans:** 37.6% of total volume (Average recycled aluminum scrap content: 72.4%).
- **One-Way Non-Returnable Glass:** 9.8% of total volume.
- **PET Plastic Bottles:** 4.4% of total volume (Food-grade rPET content averaged 34.1% due to severe regional market supply constraints).


## Exhibit 4: Greenhouse Gas Emissions Inventory Breakdown (2024)
- **Scope 1 (Direct Operations):** 6.8% of corporate total (brewing boilers, mobile combustion, fugitive refrigerants).
- **Scope 2 (Purchased Electricity & Steam):** 8.0% of corporate total (purchased utility electricity and district heat).
- **Scope 3 (Value Chain):** 85.2% of corporate total.
  * **Packaging Materials & Manufacturing:** 38.4% of total carbon footprint.
  * **Agricultural Raw Materials (Barley & Hops):** 24.1% of total carbon footprint.
  * **Point-of-Sale Product Cooling & Dispense:** 17.5% of total carbon footprint.
  * **Downstream Distribution Logistics & Freight:** 5.2% of total carbon footprint.
