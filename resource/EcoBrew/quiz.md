## Q4: Streamline One vs. Streamline Two Supply Chain Architecture
- **Type**: Open-Ended
- **Hint**: Reflect on how lean cost discipline in the core manufacturing engine provides self-funded investment capital for green innovations.
- **Official Answer**: Streamline One is the core efficiency engine driving lean manufacturing and operational cost savings, while Streamline Two is the circular and sustainable supply chain directly funded by Streamline One's cost reductions, creating a self-sustaining ambidextrous operational flywheel.

### Question
Analyze the strategic relationship between 'Streamline One' and 'Streamline Two' in EcoBrew's supply chain architecture. How do operational efficiency gains in Streamline One enable the environmental and circular initiatives of Streamline Two?

### Answer (Rubric)
- Streamline One is the core linear manufacturing, lean operations, and logistics engine driving unit cost savings and gross margin improvements
- Streamline Two is the digital, circular, and sustainable supply chain governing packaging circularity, renewable energy, and upcycling ventures
- Direct cost savings generated by Streamline One self-fund Streamline Two projects without requiring external debt or dilutive capital

## Q5: Open Intellectual Property Policy & HydroVapor Recuperation
- **Type**: Open-Ended
- **Hint**: Assess how sharing thermal energy-saving IP drives industry-wide emissions reductions while EcoBrew's competitive advantage remains rooted in brand and distribution scale.
- **Official Answer**: Licensing HydroVapor Recuperation for free was strategically sound because it drives sector-wide brewing decarbonization and cements ESG leadership, while EcoBrew's competitive advantage and economic moat rely on global distribution scale, brand affinity, and supply chain execution rather than proprietary boiling equipment.

### Question
Evaluate EcoBrew's strategic decision to license its patented 'HydroVapor Recuperation' (HVR) brewing technology for free to external craft breweries. Was this decision strategically justified for an ESG industry leader? Justify your position using case facts.

### Answer (Rubric)
- HydroVapor Recuperation (HVR) bubbles micro-jets of inert nitrogen through boiling wort to reduce kettle thermal heat requirements by 65%
- Open-licensing accelerates sector-wide decarbonization across the brewing industry and solidifies ESG leadership and regulatory goodwill
- EcoBrew's core competitive advantage and economic moat stem from global distribution scale, brand loyalty, and supply chain agility rather than proprietary boiling IP
